━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CORE CONCEPT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This is NOT a normal photo gallery.

The Photos page is a cinematic interactive meme archive containing goofy photographs of the subject.

Every photograph has a meme caption.

The interface should look premium, modern, cinematic and editorial.

The CONTENT is ridiculous.
The UI should NOT look ridiculous.

The visual joke should be:

"An expensive experimental photography website was created to document this absolute idiot."

BACKGROUND PHOTO CONCEPT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use previous photographs as part of the environment.

The previous photograph should remain visible behind the current photograph.

Background photograph:

- enlarged
- heavily blurred
- darkened
- low opacity
- slightly scaled
- visually subordinate

Current photograph:

- sharp
- high contrast
- larger visual priority
- clearly separated from background
- centered or slightly offset depending on composition

Visual concept:

        PREVIOUS PHOTO
        blurred / dark
        full environment

              ↓

        CURRENT PHOTO
        sharp / clear
        foreground

When the user moves to the next photograph:

CURRENT PHOTO
      ↓
moves backward
      ↓
becomes blurred background

NEXT PHOTO
      ↓
moves forward
      ↓
becomes sharp foreground

The transition should feel like moving through a stack of photographs.

ANIMATION DIRECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The photo transition should feel cinematic.

When changing images:

Current:
opacity 100%
blur 0
scale 1

Previous:
moves backward
blur increases
opacity decreases
scale increases slightly

Next:
starts slightly smaller
moves forward
becomes sharp
opacity increases

Use smooth movement.

Avoid:
- bouncing
- spinning
- flashy zoom
- excessive parallax
- random particles
- 3D effects

The animation should feel like physical photographs moving through space.
